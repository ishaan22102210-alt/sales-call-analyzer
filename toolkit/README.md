# Sales toolkit

Tools for selling AssemblyAI (speech AI for developers), in the order you'd use them around a call.

| When | Tool | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Before the call | [call-plan.md](call-plan.md) | 10-minute prep sheet: who they are, what you think hurts, your goal |
| On the call | [discovery-questions.md](discovery-questions.md) | The questions that uncover pain, cost, decision makers and timing |
| On the call | [objection-handling.md](objection-handling.md) | What to say to "Whisper is free", "we use Deepgram", "send me info"... |
| On the call | [roi_calculator.py](roi_calculator.py) | Build vs buy: what self-hosting really costs compared with an API |
| After the call | [follow-up-email.md](follow-up-email.md) | Recap email that gets the next meeting |
| After the call | `python analyze.py calls/x.m4a` | Grade the call, then fix one thing next time |

## The loop

    plan the call ──> run it (record) ──> analyze it ──> follow up ──> change ONE thing ──> repeat

## Before you use any fact on a real call

Check AssemblyAI's current pricing, models and accuracy numbers on [assemblyai.com](https://www.assemblyai.com) and in the docs. They change often, and quoting an old number to a real buyer costs you trust.
