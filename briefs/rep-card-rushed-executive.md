**Difficulty:** hard

## For the REP (read this before you start)

You sell speech-to-text for AssemblyAI. Your only goal is to **earn a second meeting**. You will not close a deal in 5 minutes.

**Tips:**
1. **Respect the time limit right away.** For example: "I'll keep it to five minutes. Can I ask two quick questions to see if this is even worth your time?"
2. **Ask about the business, not the technology.** "What happens when a transcript is wrong?" works better than "What's your word error rate?"
3. **Find the pain, then its cost.** Once they name a problem, ask what it costs them.
4. **Handle the "Whisper is free" objection by asking a question**, not arguing. For example: "Free to license, sure. What does it cost you to run and maintain?"
5. **Ask for one small, specific next step before time runs out**, with a name and a time. For example: "Could I get 30 minutes with whoever owns your speech pipeline next week?"

**After the call, check your report for:**
- talk ratio under 40%
- no monologue over 30 seconds
- MEDDPICC elements covered: Identify pain, Metrics, Economic buyer, Competition
- a concrete next step agreed
